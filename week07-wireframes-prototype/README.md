## 2. Three Design Requirements

Translate your **top three Week 6 priorities** into exactly three interface requirements.

### Requirement 1

**Week 6 problem: Unclear CTA**  
**Design requirement: Simple but attention grabbing section guiding user to find their preferred trail**  
**How this helps Maya: This will help Maya with starting her goal of searching for a trail that is easy for her to traverse**

### Requirement 2

**Week 6 problem: No clear way to discern trail time and difficulty**  
**Design requirement: Add estimated trail completion time, and difficulty elements to trail cards**  
**How this helps Maya: This will help Maya with her goal of finding a trail that can be completed in three hours or less, and is easy to complete**

### Requirement 3

**Week 6 problem: No GPS or map information**  
**Design requirement: Add a link or more trail details button that displays GPS location, as well as a map of the trail**  
**How this helps Maya: This will help Maya since she tends to take screenshots of important information for when there is no service, and with GPS data she can see if there are any reviews or images of the trail she is heading to, and so she can share it with her hiking buddy**
